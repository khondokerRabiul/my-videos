# STORY WORLD

The voice and the visuals tell one story: **a machine that reconstructs
attention itself** — not a copy of what's already viral, but the underlying
geometry of why people watch. Scenes progress from "beneath the noise"
(starfield) → the idea taking shape (wireframe globe forming) → the
craft (engineered cuts, monolith grid) → the drop (hyperspace burst +
title). Keep every script line in that arc; OpenCode can rewrite
config.json and the same world renders anyway.
