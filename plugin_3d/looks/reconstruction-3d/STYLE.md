# STYLE — reconstruction-3d

The look reconstructs "attention" in three dimensions:

- **Palette** — deep navy base (#090C1F→#2A0F3E), horizontal scan of slow sine-wave
  light, per-scene accent tints: teal (hook), deep blue (reveal), amber (proof),
  warm white (outro).
- **3D base layer** — starfield tunnel with motion streaks; perspective floor grid;
  rotating wireframe globe with a burning core; monolith cubes; hyperspace outro.
- **Typography** — angular stroke-style caps, auto-fitted to 86% frame width,
  scale-in + fade per scene, exact 3.4s hold.
- **Film feel** — vignette, sparse grain, boundary flashes, progress bar, HUD
  (`3D RECON`, `REC`), hard fade-in/out to black.
- **Audio bed** — free SFX (procedural WAVs): whoosh at scene boundaries, boom,
  sparkle, tick, ding, click. 0.55s voiceless "cinematic beat" between scenes.

Rules: never compete with the voice; accents stay in one family per scene;
every element must move (nothing static > 2s).
